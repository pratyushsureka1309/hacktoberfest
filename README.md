# hacktoberfest
Participated in a really fun and uniquely designed CP contest today! The contest had **3 rounds**, each with a different style of problem-solving, and honestly, what made it interesting was how every round tested something different. Round 1 was regular competitive programming, with challenging problems that tested our logic, implementation skills, and ability to think under time pressure. It was a great way to get started before things got a lot more interesting in the next rounds.

**Round 2 was probably the most unique part of the contest.** You and your teammate had to solve the same problem, but with a twist: for the first 10 minutes, one person solved it while the other was blind, and for the next 10 minutes, the roles were reversed. It made communication, planning, and trusting your teammate just as important as writing the code itself. Round 3 took things up another level with harder questions and some pretty challenging constraints, including recursion-based problems and a limit of fewer than 30 lines of code. These restrictions made us think beyond the usual approaches and come up with creative solutions instead of just relying on brute force.

Overall, it was a genuinely refreshing experience compared to a regular CP contest. The problems were definitely worth the challenge, and the different formats made every round memorable in its own way. We managed to **win the contest as a team**, which made the experience even better! More than the result, it was great to see how problem-solving, teamwork, and thinking outside the box all came together. Definitely a contest worth participating in
